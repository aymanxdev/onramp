YOUR ROUTE

Tidy my inbox every morning and tell me what needs a reply.

COWORK + Microsoft 365 · scheduled task

Why this

  • Cowork can hold a job that repeats, so you set it up once rather than asking each day.
  • Your mail already lives in Microsoft 365, and there is a connector for it.
  • Nothing here needs code, so you can undo it from the same screen you set it up in.

Set up

{{ setupCount }}

Watch out for

  • !It will not send replies unless you ask it to. Keep it read only until you trust the summary.
  • !Very large mailboxes get truncated. Give it a window, not your whole archive.

Plan note

Connectors and scheduled tasks sit on the paid personal plans (as of 7 September 2026). Plans change, so check the current list before you commit.

OUTLOOK

If you would rather stay inside your mail client, use Claude in Outlook and ask for the same summary by hand each morning. Slower, but nothing to connect.

Start with this prompt

Every weekday at 08:00, read my unread mail from the last 24 hours. Group it into three lists: needs a reply today, waiting on someone else, and safe to archive. For each item give me the sender, one line of context, and a suggested next step. Keep the whole thing under [300 words] and flag anything from [my manager] first.
